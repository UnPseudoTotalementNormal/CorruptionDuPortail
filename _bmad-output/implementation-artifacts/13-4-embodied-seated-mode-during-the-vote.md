# Story 13.4: Embodied seated mode during the Vote

Status: done

## Story

As a developer (Poyo),
I want each player snapped into their seat at the table during the **Vote** — able to look around slightly and stay present, but unable to move — with the existing vote UI intact,
so that the Vote becomes a face-to-face discussion moment without losing the current voting mechanics.

> **Epic 13 — Player Embodiment & Physical Presence.** Builds directly on **Story 13.3** (the `AvatarCameraArbiter` that routes `VoteState → CameraMode.Embodied`) and **Story 13.1** (the seat registry `AvatarManager.GetSeat(clientId)`). **This story fills in the `Embodied` slot the 13.3 arbiter already routes to** — it adds the concrete **seated camera**, the **body seat-snap**, and the **clamped look**. 13.3 left a documented fall-back ("Embodied keeps the unchanged board camera + movement locked until 13.4"); **13.4 replaces that fall-back with a real embodied presentation** by adding one new camera component the arbiter drives, **without touching the arbiter's state-reaction wiring** (NFR2). No voice (13.5/13.6). The Vote *mechanics* (`VoteState`) are **not modified** — embodiment is presentation-only.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **Seated on entry, movement locked.** When `VoteState` is entered (arbiter resolves `CameraMode.Embodied`), each player's avatar is **seated facing the table** and **movement is locked** (the Story 13.2 controller is already suppressed by the 13.3 arbiter's `SetMovementEnabled(false)` — 13.4 must not regress that). The local player views the table from **their own seat** (DO3 — "front seat from their own viewpoint").
2. **Seating route = (A), documented.** The chosen route is **(A) fixed global seats + the embodied camera placed at the local client's own seat** — avatars keep **one shared `NetworkTransform` world position** (each owner moves its own body to its **global** seat, identical on every client) and the **local embodied camera is placed at the local seat**, rather than **(B)** per-client rotated avatar presentation. Route A is network-clean (no per-client desync of avatar world positions); it is documented in the code. Each avatar's global seat is resolved through the existing **stable** `AvatarManager.GetSeat(clientId)` registry (Story 13.1) so every client agrees on where every avatar sits.
3. **Clamped look.** The local player has **clamped look control: yaw ±75°, pitch ±40°** (approx, **tunable** `[SerializeField]` placeholders — Poyo owns the final feel). The clamp is **relative to the seat's facing** (looking "around the table" from the seat). The look **binding** (free mouse vs hold-to-look) is documented in the code; default ships as free-mouse reading the existing Input System **`Look`** action.
4. **Vote UI + mechanics untouched (presentation-only).** The **existing per-card vote UI continues to function** (cast one vote / skip, the early-finish-when-all-voted and tie/skip resolution rules in `VoteState` are **unchanged**). **`VoteState.cs` is NOT edited** — embodiment mutates **no** vote state, no game state, and adds no RPC. The existing vote PlayMode/EditMode tests (`VoteTally` golden, `VoteState` flow) stay **green unchanged**.
4b. **Coexistence with `BoardCameraManager` (NFR1).** The embodied camera **coexists** with `BoardCameraManager` exactly like the 13.2 `AvatarFollowCamera`: it **outranks** the board cameras via Cinemachine **priority** while active and **stands down** (drops priority) on exit — board cameras are **never** removed or disabled. The arbiter's existing `Avatar` board-source toggle (already `false` in Embodied from 13.3 → arrow neighbour-nav cut) is **untouched**.
5. **Restored on exit (NFR1).** On **leaving `VoteState`** (arbiter resolves `Board` for `VoteRecapState` and everything after), the embodied camera **stands down** and the prior fixed-camera presentation is **restored**; `VoteRecapState` and everything after look **visually unchanged**. Movement stays locked outside the Lobby (there is no return-to-lobby mid-match — documented; the arbiter already handles re-enable generically).
6. **NFR2 — transition ordering intact.** The embodiment transition **reuses the 13.3 arbiter** and introduces **no** new dependence on the state-index ordering. The arbiter stays a pure **reaction** to `currentGameStateIndex.OnValueChanged` (never writes the index). The refactor's **Story 2.11a sequence golden stays green unchanged** (`Assets/Scripts/Tests/PlayMode/GameLogic/GameStates/GameLoopTransitionOrderingTests.cs` + `Assets/Scripts/Tests/Editor/GameLoopMachineTests.cs`).
7. **Tests + clean console.** A **PlayMode test** asserts: on `VoteState` (Embodied) entry the embodied camera goes active + movement is locked + the local avatar body is at its seat; on exit (Board) the embodied camera stands down. An **EditMode golden** pins the **look-clamp math** (a pure POCO) at the yaw/pitch boundaries. The 3 DI/census guards (`DiSeamNoLocatorGuard` / `SceneWiringGuard` / `StaticSingletonCensusGuard`) stay **green**. Full EditMode + PlayMode suite green, console clean, GameScene boot smoke clean. Baseline at story start: **EM 221 / PM 160** (Story 13.3 end-state). Visual seated feel is **golden-blind** → flag for a Poyo playtest.

### The DO3 seating route — (A), and why

> **Route A is mandated by the epic's resolved DO3** ("prefer (A) fixed global seats + the embodied camera placed at the local client's own seat … over (B) per-client rotated avatar presentation"). Implement A; document it; do **not** implement B.

- **A — what ships:** each owner moves **its own** avatar body to **its own global seat** (`GetSeat(ownerClientId)`), so every client sees the same world positions for every avatar (one shared `NetworkTransform` position per avatar — network-clean). The local player's **camera** is placed at the **local** seat → it naturally faces the table and sees the others across it. "Front seat from your viewpoint" (DO3) is achieved purely by **camera placement**, not by rotating avatars or the scene.
- **Why not B:** per-client rotated avatar presentation means each client sees avatars at different world positions → desync risk + heavier. The epic explicitly prefers A.
- **Stable seat index:** `AvatarManager.SeatIndexForClient` keys off the **replicated avatar-list order** (`AvatarManager.cs:278-289`), identical on every client. A mid-match disconnect re-indexes later avatars (documented in 13.1 as acceptable for this slice — no mid-match join/leave reseat is in scope).

### `CharacterController` seat-teleport gotcha (Task 3)

A `CharacterController` continuously asserts its own position and will **silently override** a direct `transform.position = …` write. The correct pattern (document it inline):

```csharp
_characterController.enabled = false;
transform.SetPositionAndRotation(_position, _rotation);
_characterController.enabled = true;
```

Owner-only (`IsOwner`) — a non-owner can't move an owner-auth `NetworkTransform`. Re-enabling resumes the (currently disabled-by-arbiter) movement integration without moving the body.
